Our Services.

Specialized Consulting for Organizations Where Leadership, Risk, and Resilience Matter Most.

We work alongside public safety agencies, aviation organizations, and other high-responsibility industries to develop tailored solutions informed by firsthand experience. Our approach brings practical insight and strategic perspective to the decisions that matter most.

01Executive Leadership & AdvisoryStrengthening leadership at every level. 7 services

Senior counsel for leaders navigating organizational change, succession and high-stakes decisions, grounded in firsthand executive experience in public safety and aviation.

  • Senior Leadership Advisory & Executive Coaching

    Confidential counsel for chiefs, directors and executive teams facing high-stakes decisions.

  • Organizational Assessments & Strategic Planning

    Independent review of structure, operations and performance, with a practical plan to move forward.

  • Succession Planning & Leadership Transitions

    Preparing the next generation of leaders and preserving institutional knowledge.

  • Fire & EMS Program Development

    Building, evaluating and strengthening fire and EMS programs, including accreditation readiness.

  • Grant Strategy, Writing & Administration

    Identifying funding, preparing competitive applications and managing awards.

  • Capital Projects, Station Design & Fleet

    Guidance on station construction, capital improvement and apparatus planning.

  • Expert Witness & Litigation Support

    Case review, written opinions and testimony on fire service operations, EMS standard of care, evacuation and emergency planning.

02Strategic Communications & Public RelationsBuilding trust. Strengthening reputations. Advancing missions. 5 services

Protecting public trust before, during and after a crisis through strategic communications, reputation management and public affairs.

  • Crisis Communications Planning & Response

    Plans, protocols and counsel so leaders communicate clearly when it matters most.

  • Reputation Management & Public Affairs

    Protecting public trust and building relationships with officials, partners and the community.

  • Executive Communications & Media Preparation

    Messaging, briefings and media training for leaders who speak for their organization.

  • Stakeholder & Community Engagement

    Structured outreach to the people your decisions affect.

  • Public Education & Awareness Campaigns

    Campaigns that inform the public and support safety and preparedness goals.

03Risk, Readiness & ResilienceAnticipating challenges. Strengthening preparedness. Building resilience. 5 services

Identifying vulnerabilities, strengthening preparedness and building the capacity organizations need to anticipate, absorb and recover from disruption.

  • Emergency Response Plans

    Development, gap analysis against national and state standards, and annual updates.

  • Tabletop Exercises

    Scenario-based exercises built on the Incident Command System, with after-action reports and corrective action plans.

  • Incident Command & Evacuation Training

    Preparing staff to operate within the Incident Command System and carry out evacuations.

  • Wildland-Urban Interface Resilience

    Risk assessment and preparedness planning for communities, developments and associations at the wildland edge.

  • Event & Venue Emergency Planning

    Safety and emergency planning for large events and high-occupancy venues.
